About Robert

A chef's training, a lifetime of the highest standards, and now wild British game.

After school Robert studied at Westminster Catering College and Hendon Polytechnic, then headed to Ecole Les Roches in the Swiss Alps, where he was best in his year for butchery, first out of 300. He went on to work in Montreux and Zurich, and spent two years in the Cathay Pacific catering kitchens in Hong Kong.

At 17 he worked under Bruno Marti at La Belle Auberge, who led Canada's Culinary Olympic team. Later, Chef John Nocita taught him Italian cured meats: salami and air-cured hams. Both had an incessant drive for freshness and quality.

Robert then founded and ran his own private jet catering company, serving private aircraft from every airport VIP terminal across the south, where only the best would do and discretion mattered as much as the food.

That is the standard every piece of game gets in his curing room in Hampshire today.
